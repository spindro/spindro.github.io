AAF 1149: Earning 3 credits

To obtain the 3 credits for AAF activities, students must complete both components:

  1. One Training Camp (compulsory; no substitutions permitted).
  2. One elective component:
    • A Hackathon
    • A second Training Camp
    • An internship or stage of at least 60 hours. The host company must differ from the company selected for the thesis internship.

Submission procedure

When registering for the exam, email me with the subject line [AAF] and include:

  • A list of your completed qualifying activities
  • All relevant completion certificates as attachments

Students who do not follow this procedure will not be registered.
